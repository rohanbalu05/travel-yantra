*Legend.* The locals tell a story of how this ground got its name, a tale of a grand victory born from terror. The town became Mahishuru, the demon's land, which speech eventually softened to Mysuru [5]. It is a strange habit of this city to keep the name of its tormentor, put the victorious goddess on the hill, and throw a massive party for her triumph every autumn [5].
