*Legend.* People here say the very ground you stand on was won in blood when the goddess Chamundeshwari descended to the hill above to slay the buffalo demon Mahishasura [2]. The story goes that this ancient victory gave the city its name, wearing down over centuries from Mahishuru to Mysuru [2]. While historians argue the name simply means "buffalo country" and predates any legend, the local sellers do not care about the dispute [2]. They still place fresh red kumkum powder from the market on their thresholds every morning to honor her victory, turning a cosmic battle into a daily household chore [2, 9].
