What Travel Yantra is

Travel Yantra plans trips in India, tells the story of the places on them, and sends you to the right seller to book. It is a planning and guide service, not a travel agent: we do not sell tickets, rooms or tours.

Your account

You sign in with your mobile number or a Google account. Keep access to them safe; anything done in your account is treated as done by you. You must be 18 or older, or use Travel Yantra with a parent or guardian who agrees on your behalf.

Plans and prices

Plans are suggestions. Before you see one we check opening hours, closing days, travel times, meals and your budget against the best information we have, but places change their hours and fares change by the minute. Confirm timings with the place and prices with the seller before you rely on them. Every price we show is an estimate until the seller confirms it.

Katha

Katha tells history and legend side by side, and labels which is which. History comes from the sources listed under each story. Legends are told as stories people tell, not as fact, and are never presented as the only view of a faith or a community. If you spot a mistake, use Report on the story and we will check it.

Bookings

When you book, you buy from the seller: IRCTC for trains, the airline for flights, the hotel or booking site for stays. Their prices, rules, cancellations and refunds apply, and they are responsible for the booking. If you add a PNR or booking ID, we will watch its status for you as a convenience; the seller's own status is always the one that counts.

Using it fairly

  • No scraping, bulk automated requests or attempts to get around limits.
  • No using the guide for anything other than travel. It will politely decline.
  • No content that harms, harasses or deceives anyone, including in reviews or reports.

Limits and law

To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for delays, cancellations or losses caused by sellers, weather, closures or events outside our control. These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Bengaluru have jurisdiction. We will tell you in the app at least 14 days before any important change.

Privacy Policy

Written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. In short: we collect what planning needs, keep it in India, and delete it when you ask.

Who looks after your data

Travel Yantra, run by its project team in Bengaluru, decides how your personal data is used. Our Grievance Officer is [NAME], reachable at [GRIEVANCE EMAIL]. We reply within 7 days and resolve within 30.

What we collect

  • Account: your mobile number or Google email, your name and your language.
  • Trips: places, dates, how many people travel and their age groups, food and pace preferences, and your budget. Traveller names only if you add them to book.
  • Booking references: PNRs and booking IDs you add, so we can track them.
  • Voice: recordings you send are turned into text, then deleted within 24 hours.
  • Location: only while Katha by location is on, used on your phone to find the nearest place. We do not keep a history of where you have been.
  • Service logs: errors and response times, kept for 30 days.

Why we use it

To understand your request, build and check your plan, tell your Katha, track bookings you added and keep the service running. We do not sell personal data, we show no advertising, and we do not use your trips to train AI models.

Who else sees it

  • Google Cloud runs Travel Yantra for us in its Mumbai region, including the AI models that read your request and write your plan, and the maps.
  • Sellers see trip details only when you choose to book with them. For IRCTC you copy the details across yourself.
  • WhatsApp carries your messages only if you use Travel Yantra on WhatsApp.

How long we keep it

Your account until you delete it. Each trip until you delete it. Booking references until 30 days after the trip ends. Voice recordings for up to 24 hours. Service logs for 30 days. When you delete your account, everything is erased within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.

Your rights

You can see a summary of your data and who it was shared with, correct it, erase it, withdraw consent, and name someone to act for you if you cannot. Most of this is one tap away in Settings. For anything else, write to the Grievance Officer. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

We need a parent or guardian's consent to process data of anyone under 18. We do not track children's behaviour or show them targeted content.

Security and changes

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only the people who run the service can reach it. If a breach ever affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires. If this policy changes, we will show you what changed and ask again where your consent is needed.
