How it works

From one sentence to a trip you can take.

Talk to it the way you would talk to a friend who knows India well. The AI listens, compares and explains. Our planner checks every stop before you see the plan.

01

Say it the way you would say it to a friend.

Type or speak in any of six languages, or mix them. Dates, people, budget, food and pace are picked out for you to check.

02

Watch it plan.

Four specialists work at once: transport, places, stays and stories. Every stop is checked against opening hours, closing days, travel time, meals and your budget.

03

Pick a way to go.

Road, rail or air, compared on time, cost and comfort for the people you are travelling with, with a clear recommendation and the reason for it.

04

Change anything by asking.

Say it should be lighter, cheaper or more about temples. Only the day you touched is rebuilt, and you are told exactly what moved.

05

Book with the official sellers.

Trains on IRCTC, with every detail ready to copy across. Flights and stays on the airline and booking sites, opened with your dates filled in. Add the PNR and we keep watch.

06

Go with a guide that tells the story.

Each place plays its Katha as you arrive, in your language, saved for when there is no signal.
